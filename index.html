Zhixuan Shen

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence, Southwest Jiaotong University. I am advised by Prof. Tianrui Li and jointly advised by Prof. Joey Tianyi Zhou. My research focuses on Embodied Navigation, especially object-goal navigation, multi-robot semantic navigation, visual-language reasoning, and efficient robotic decision making.

My work aims to build embodied agents that can perceive, reason, and navigate in complex indoor environments by combining semantic maps, multimodal foundation models, and interpretable decision strategies.

Research Interests

  • Embodied Navigation: object-goal navigation, visual semantic navigation, and long-horizon planning in unknown environments.
  • Multimodal Reasoning for Robotics: vision-language models, multimodal chain-of-thought reasoning, and interpretable navigation decisions.
  • Multi-Robot Collaboration: semantic-map sharing, collaborative exploration, and efficient multi-agent navigation.
